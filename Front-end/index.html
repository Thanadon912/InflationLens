              03 · What the engine does

              Built to disagree with itself.

              Most inflation tools pick one series and print it. Inflation Lens runs every published measure in parallel, aligns them on one calendar, and treats the disagreement between them as the primary output rather than noise to be smoothed away.

              01

              Every measure, in parallel

              CPI-U, CPI-W, chained CPI, PCE, core PCE, trimmed-mean PCE and PPI final demand, computed on the same date axis. Where they diverge, the engine says by how much and which component is responsible.

              02

              Down to the basket line

              Every headline decomposes into its components with weights attached — shelter, energy, food, services — so you can see that two-thirds of a monthly print came from one line, and rebuild the index with your own basket.

              03

              Read at the level you live at

              National, regional and modelled state estimates, with revisions kept in view. Every figure carries its publisher, reference period and release date, and modelled values are never printed as observed.

              01

              Ingest

              Seven publishers polled on their own release calendars. Raw releases stored verbatim, never overwritten.

              02

              Align

              Reference periods, seasonal adjustment and base years reconciled onto one axis so measures are comparable.

              03

              Decompose

              Each index broken into components with published weights, so contribution is attributable, not asserted.

              04

              Model

              Sub-national estimates attributed from published regional series. Flagged as modelled everywhere they appear.

              05

              Publish

              Every figure leaves with its source, period, release date and revision history attached. No orphan numbers.

              04 · Where every number comes from

              The whole ledger, open.

              Inflation Lens publishes no proprietary index. Every figure on this page and in the engine traces to an official statistical release, listed here with its series, coverage and calendar. If you can't reproduce one of our numbers from these sources, that is a bug.

              PublisherSeries drawnCoverageLatest releaseNext
              Known limits, stated plainly No statistical agency publishes state-level CPI. Where this page shades individual states, the value is that state's census region's own published BLS regional CPI — not a per-state estimate — and it is labelled modelled wherever it appears, because attributing a whole region's figure to one state is still an interpretive step. The world figure is deliberately left unavailable rather than shown as a computed average: it would require population and GDP-PPP weights this engine does not yet ingest, and an unweighted number under a weighted label would be worse than none. Figures for the past ten to twelve months are subject to revision by their publishers; the engine keeps every prior vintage rather than silently restating history.
